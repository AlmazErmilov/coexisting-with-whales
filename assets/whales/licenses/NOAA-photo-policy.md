# NOAA photo reuse notice

Policy checked on 2026-10-07 at https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/website-policies-and-disclaimers#copyright-policy.

The selected NOAA photographs are credited as NOAA creations. NOAA's photo policy says its own photographs are not subject to copyright in the United States and can be used without permission, with NOAA credited. The policy treats third party photographs separately and requires obtaining permission from the named creator. Merely appearing on a NOAA website does not make a photograph public domain.
