# Whale photographs and species facts

Verified on 2026-10-07. The 15 photographs cover 15 of the 25 keys in `COMMON_NAMES` and 15 of the 21 species in the current dataset (9940 of 9996 observations). These are reference photographs of the species, taken in different parts of the world. They are not photographs of the app observations or evidence of Norwegian occurrence. Two show captive animals, identified below.

Each local JPEG is at most 240 pixels on its longest edge. The photographs total 174,420 bytes (170.3 KiB). They were resized and recompressed without other edits. The app displays a 44 pixel square crop. The full image and attribution are available in [the credits gallery](whale-images.html). [Machine readable credits](../assets/whales/credits.json) include original URLs, exact credits, taxon checks, changes and local SHA-256 hashes.

## Integration contract

Import `whaleThumbnail` and `initWhaleImages` from `js/whale-images.js`. Pass an exact scientific name; surrounding whitespace is accepted. `whaleThumbnail(name)` returns escaped HTML for one local, lazy loaded 44 × 44 image. It uses `whale-thumbnail`, `data-whale-image` (`photo` or `illustration`), `data-whale-species` and `data-whale-common`. Invoke `initWhaleImages()` once, or pass a DOM root. A capturing error listener supports dynamically inserted images and repeated calls on the same root are safe. A failed placeholder does not trigger repeated replacements.

Import `WHALE_INFO` and `getWhaleInfo` from `js/whale-info.js`. All 25 authoritative keys return `{ commonName }`; the 15 covered keys also return `fact` and `source`. Unknown or non-string input returns `null`. No fact should be rendered for profiles without `fact`. Records and the exported registry are frozen.

The consuming UI must expose a link to [photo credits](whale-images.html), alongside or near the species view, so visitors can reach source links, licenses and modification notices. Each thumbnail's title also retains author, license and source. UI wiring, style and offline cache registration belong to the integrating worker. The modules make no remote image requests.

## Research limits

Commons metadata requests were stopped after HTTP 429 responses. Already retrieved Commons metadata and credited NOAA photographs supplied the final selection. Scientific names in `COMMON_NAMES` remain the app identifiers, even where external taxonomy may use a different genus. No substitute taxa, population status or local habitat claims were introduced. The images illustrate species, including some Arctic or uncommon species; the occurrence data remain a separate source.
