# Contributor covenant code of conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologising for mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the broader conservation community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others’ private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, the code itself) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Reports may be sent to almaz.ermilov@gmail.com. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
